Setting up

Copy the puzzle box from the game: click each tile until it has the right colour, and do the same for the four corner squares. The solver runs after every change and lists the shortest set of presses it finds, up to 15 presses.

What each tile does when pressed

TileEffect
GGrayNothing.
OOrangeTakes the colour most of its side neighbours share, when there is one.
YYellowSwaps with the tile above it. Nothing on the top row.
UPurpleSwaps with the tile below it. Nothing on the bottom row.
KBlackMoves every tile in its row one place right; the last one wraps to the start.
PPinkTurns the ring of tiles around it one step clockwise.
WWhiteTurns itself and its white side neighbours gray, and its gray side neighbours white.
BBlueDoes what the centre tile does, from its own place. Copying white, it turns gray neighbours blue.

Keyboard

  • Tab moves between tiles and corners; Enter or Space steps to the next colour.
  • A colour's letter sets it on the tile or corner that has focus or is under the pointer: O orange, Y yellow, B blue, K black, G gray, P pink, U purple, W white.
  • Esc closes this window.
